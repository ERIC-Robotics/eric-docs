# Safety Guidelines

!!! danger "Read Before Operating"
    All operators must read and understand these safety guidelines before operating S.A.A.R.T.H.I.

## General Safety

- Never operate the system without valid corridor access permissions
- Always coordinate with station master and obtain block/OHE clearance
- Wear mandatory PPE: high-visibility vest, safety shoes, hard hat
- Maintain communication with control room at all times
- Know the emergency stop procedure before starting any run

## Electrical Safety

- Do not open the compute enclosure in the field
- Handle batteries per manufacturer guidelines
- Do not operate in heavy rain or standing water

## Track Safety

- Follow all railway safety rules per the operating corridor's authority
- Clear the track immediately when block time expires
- Be aware of adjacent line traffic at all times

- **Equipment failure on track**: *Document the recovery procedure*
